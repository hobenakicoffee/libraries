## Quick orientation (read in 2 minutes)

**What it is:** Hobe Naki Coffee is a creator monetization platform built specifically for Bangladesh. Any creator — artist, writer, fitness trainer, YouTuber — can sign up with just their email and start earning in BDT within minutes, with no trade license, company registration, or paperwork required.

**Core products (four):**
- **Coffee Gifts** — supporters send 1–100 "coffees" (tips) with an optional message
- **Mini Shop** — sell digital products (ebooks, templates, presets) or physical goods
- **Newsletter** — publish content with flexible free / members-only / pay-per-post access
- **Memberships** — recurring subscription plans (monthly, annual, lifetime)

**The one-line pitch:** "The first creator income platform that works the way Bangladesh works — BDT payments, no paperwork, no middlemen."

**What's live:** Coffee Gifts, Newsletter, Feed, Creator Profiles, Auth, Pricing, Contact, FAQ, Policy pages. The Shop backend is in progress on a feature branch.

**What's not yet live:** Shop checkout (in progress), recurring Coffee Gifts, multi-service membership bundling, marketplace/explore page, video/audio/live streaming.

## Important notes for the marketing team

1. **Fee structure matters.** Platform fees are waived to 0% for creators on a paid platform subscription plan. This is a genuine differentiator versus every competitor and should be a cornerstone of messaging.

2. **The name is bilingual.** "হবে নাকি Coffee?" is Bengali for "Shall we have coffee?" — the question mark is intentional and part of the brand identity. In English, the platform is referred to as "Hobe Naki Coffee" without the question mark in most contexts.

3. **Target market is very specific.** This is not a global platform positioned against Patreon. It is built for Bangladeshi creators, with BDT, Bengali language support, bKash/Nagad payments, and zero legal-setup friction. All messaging should reflect this specificity.

4. **The platform is pre-launch / early-access.** The landing page collects waitlist signups. Do not imply the platform has a large existing creator base in campaigns unless verified with real numbers from the product team.
